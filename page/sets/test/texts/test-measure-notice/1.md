# Test set: the measurement

This set is **for tests alone**: no sample is made under it, and no release carries it.

## What a sample holds

- the strokes, with the *history* of the writing — the time and the course of the pen;
- the crops and their markup;
- the writer code, such as `ABCD2345`.

| Who | Sees the sample |
|---|---|
| the organizer | yes |
| anyone else | no |

## How it ends

1. The organizer has the file.

The file goes off the device then.

2. A point numbered as the text numbers it.
